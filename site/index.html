Guides

Write a how-to without writing

Press ⌃⌥G and do the task as usual. Every click becomes a step with a screenshot and a marker on what you clicked; typing in a field becomes one step.

  • Drag steps to reorder, give each a title and description
  • Ring, dot, pointer, hand, spotlight or step-number markers
  • Typed text in captions is optional — off, it's never stored

What people use it for

Hide what's private

Press B and drag over emails, names or tokens. Pixelate or blur — and fix a missed spot later.

Walk someone through a UI

Press N and click each control: 1, 2, 3. Export a one-page PDF.

Give review feedback

Drop ✓, 👍, ? and ! stamps, magnify the small print, paste into the thread.

Record a quick demo

MP4 or GIF with pause, resume and a cursor highlight. Trim it, or save just the trimmed part as a GIF.

Copy text out of anything

Video calls, images, error dialogs. Built on the OCR inside macOS and Windows.

Before / after collages

Start from a blank canvas, paste or drag in screenshots side by side, label them, export.

Every export format

PNG, JPEG, PDF, WebP, TIFF or BMP — or just ⌘C and paste.

A library of everything

The name

Rega is Hebrew for “a moment”

In Israel you hear it all day: “Rega!” — hang on a second. “Rega, rega…” — wait, let me show you. “Rak rega” — just a moment.

That's how long it should take to show someone what's on your screen: one shortcut, one drag, and it's on their screen too. Just a rega.

Wait, it's Hebrew? If that has you warming up for “from the river to the sea”, relax: the only thing here that goes from end to end is a scrolling capture. Closing this tab takes about a rega.
