Capture

Land on the exact pixel

Rega freezes the screen while you select, so menus and hover states stay put.

  • Live size label, crosshair and pixel magnifier with the colour's hex code
  • Click a window to grab just that window
  • 3, 5 or 10 second delay to open a menu first
  • On your clipboard the moment you let go
Guides

Write a how-to without writing

Press ⌃⌥G and do the task as usual. Every click becomes a step with a screenshot and a marker on what you clicked; typing in a field becomes one step.

  • Drag steps to reorder, give each a title and description
  • Ring, dot, pointer, hand, spotlight or step-number markers
  • Typed text in captions is optional — off, it's never stored
Share

One small page that plays like stories

A guide is a single HTML file with every screenshot inside. Mail it, upload it, or open it in any browser — offline, on phones, in light or dark mode.

  • Back / Next, arrow keys, and a link to each step
  • Five steps over four screens: about 800 KB
  • No account and no upload — the file is yours

The name

Rega is Hebrew for “a moment”

In Israel you hear it all day: “Rega!” — hang on a second. “Rega, rega…” — wait, let me show you. “Rak rega” — just a moment.

That's how long it should take to show someone what's on your screen: one shortcut, one drag, and it's on their screen too. Just a rega.

Wait, it's Hebrew? If that has you warming up for “from the river to the sea”, relax: the only thing here that goes from end to end is a scrolling capture. Closing this tab takes about a rega.

  1. Open the disk image and drag Rega to Applications.

Changelog

What's new

0.4.216 Nov 2026
Latest

Update notices, and a steadier window on Windows.

  • Rega tells you when a new version is out: it checks the website once a day and shows a notice with a Download button. Turn it off in Settings › General › Updates.
  • Windows: Rega draws with DirectX 12 on the graphics card that drives your screen, to stop the window blinking blank while you hover the capture buttons on some PCs.
0.4.115 Nov 2026

Copy and paste marks in the editor.

  • Select any mark (arrow, text, shape, stamp, pasted image…) and press ⌘C / Ctrl+C, then ⌘V / Ctrl+V to drop a copy next to it. Paste again for more copies, each a little further along.
  • Copying a pasted image also puts it on the system clipboard, so other apps can take it.
0.4.011 Nov 2026

Guides: do a task once, get a step-by-step how-to.

  • Guide (⌃⌥G) turns every click into a step with a screenshot and a marker, typing in a field into one step, and shortcuts into steps of their own.
  • Reorder steps by dragging, give each a title and description, move or hide its marker.
  • Share one small HTML page that plays like stories, works offline and on phones, and follows the viewer's light or dark mode.
  • Settings › Guide: marker style and colour, step time, quality, and what recording picks up.
  • Step numbers in the editor can start at any number, or Continue at N from another image.
  • The highlighter keeps its own yellow and straightens wobbly strokes.
  • The selection and recording border turns in the logo's gradient, with rounded corners.
