macOS 12.3 or later
First launch
  1. Open the disk image and drag Rega to Applications.
  2. Open Rega. macOS says it can't check the app for malware: Rega isn't notarized by Apple yet.
  3. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.
  4. Grant Screen Recording (and Accessibility for scrolling captures and guides) when Rega asks.

0.4.115 Nov 2026
Latest

Copy and paste marks in the editor.

  • Select any mark (arrow, text, shape, stamp, pasted image…) and press ⌘C / Ctrl+C, then ⌘V / Ctrl+V to drop a copy next to it. Paste again for more copies, each a little further along.
  • Copying a pasted image also puts it on the system clipboard, so other apps can take it.
0.4.011 Nov 2026

Guides: do a task once, get a step-by-step how-to.

  • Guide (⌃⌥G) turns every click into a step with a screenshot and a marker, typing in a field into one step, and shortcuts into steps of their own.
  • Reorder steps by dragging, give each a title and description, move or hide its marker.
  • Share one small HTML page that plays like stories, works offline and on phones, and follows the viewer's light or dark mode.
  • Settings › Guide: marker style and colour, step time, quality, and what recording picks up.
  • Step numbers in the editor can start at any number, or Continue at N from another image.
  • The highlighter keeps its own yellow and straightens wobbly strokes.
  • The selection and recording border turns in the logo's gradient, with rounded corners.
0.3.05 Nov 2026

Faster recording on the system's own video codec.

  • MP4 recording, playback and trimming use the H.264 codec built into macOS and Windows, so the app is smaller.
  • Frames are scaled 6–9× faster, and a Retina full-screen recording at 30 fps fills every frame.
  • GIFs encode up to 2× faster; long GIFs open instantly in the player.
  • Windows: Rega starts on N editions too, and points you to the Media Feature Pack for MP4.
0.2.04 Nov 2026

Windows, a video player, and more tools.

  • Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit), with a branded installer.
  • A video player with trimming and Save as GIF.
  • GIF quality presets (Small, Balanced, High) and much lighter GIFs.
  • New tools: magnifier, stamps, fill and eraser, plus Flatten image.
  • A properties panel that shows only what applies to the tool or mark.
  • Select several library items with ⌘/⇧-click; an About window.
  • Recording is sturdier: quitting mid-recording keeps the video, and a full disk no longer crashes Rega.
0.1.02 Nov 2026

First preview for macOS.

  • Region, window and full-screen capture with a pixel magnifier.
  • The annotation editor: arrows, shapes, text, blur, steps, crop and more.
  • Scrolling capture, screen recording to MP4 or GIF, and grab text (OCR).
  • A library of every capture, global shortcuts and a menu bar icon.
