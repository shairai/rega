Privacy

Short version: what you capture stays on your computer, and nobody is watching.

The app

Your captures never leave your computer. Screenshots, recordings, guides and the text Rega reads from your screen are saved as files in the folder you choose in Settings, and copied to your clipboard when you ask. Rega has no account, no cloud storage and no analytics.

Recording a guide sees your clicks and typing only while the guide is recording, and only to turn them into steps. Typed text goes into captions only if you turn that on, and on macOS, typing into password fields is never seen.

The update check

Once a day, Rega downloads latest.json from this website to see whether a newer version is out. The request carries Rega's version number and nothing about you or your captures. Like any web request it comes from your IP address, which our host, Vercel, may keep in its logs for a short time.

Turn it off in Rega's Settings › General › Updates. You can still check by hand from the same place.

This website

No cookies, no analytics, no trackers, no third-party fonts or scripts. Vercel, which hosts the site, keeps standard request logs (IP address, page and time) to run and protect the service; see Vercel's privacy policy.
